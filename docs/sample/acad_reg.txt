ACADEMIC REGULATIONS FOR B.TECH PROGRAMMES (SAMPLE)
Version 3.1. Issued by the Office of the Dean (Academics).

7 ATTENDANCE
7.1 Attendance is recorded for every lecture, tutorial and laboratory session of each registered course.
7.2 Minimum attendance. A student must have a minimum attendance of 75% in each course to be permitted to appear in the end-semester examination of that course.
7.3 Detention. A student who does not meet the requirement in clause 7.2 shall be detained in that course and must re-register for it.
8 EXAMINATIONS
8.1 Each course is evaluated out of 100 marks: 40 internal marks and 60 external (end-semester) marks.
8.2 Pass mark. A student must secure at least 40% of the total marks in a course to pass that course.
8.3 A student who fails a course or is absent from its end-semester examination carries it as an active backlog.
8.4 Supplementary examination. Students who have failed a course or were absent from its end-semester examination are eligible to appear in the supplementary examination for that course. Detained students are not eligible for the supplementary examination.
8.5 Procedure for supplementary examination. (a) Log in to the student ERP portal and open Examinations, Supplementary Registration. (b) Select the backlog courses you wish to appear in. (c) Pay the fee of Rs. 1000 per course online. (d) Submit the form within 7 days of the result declaration and download the acknowledgement. Late applications are not accepted.
