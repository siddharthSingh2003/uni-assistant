TRAINING AND PLACEMENT POLICY 2025 (SAMPLE)
Training and Placement Cell.

1 Eligibility for campus placement. A student must have a minimum CGPA of 6.5 to register for campus placement.
2 A student must have no active backlogs at the time of registration for campus placement.
3 Registration procedure. Students register on the placement portal with their ERP credentials and upload a resume approved by the department coordinator.
4 A student who accepts an offer from a dream company is not eligible for further placement drives.
